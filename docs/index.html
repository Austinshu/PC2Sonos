Your computer's sound, on your Sonos

PC2Sonos streams everything your Windows PC or Mac plays to your Sonos speakers, in sync with your desk speakers, with no echo. It's free, runs on your own computer, and starts by itself when you log in.

Download free View on GitHub

Windows 10 or newer, macOS 12 or newer. No account needed.

Why your PC speakers and Sonos echo

Sonos speakers always play a second or two behind whatever sends them audio. That delay is built into Sonos so a group of speakers stays in step, and no app can remove it. So when your computer's speakers and a Sonos play the same thing, you hear it twice. PC2Sonos holds your computer's speakers back by the same amount, like the lip-sync setting on a TV or AV receiver. Press Auto once and it measures the delay for you.

What it does

Every Sonos, found for you

Finds all your speakers automatically, even on a separate network or VLAN. Turn on the ones you want, each with its own volume.

No echo

Your PC speakers are delayed to match Sonos, and it resyncs in the background so it doesn't drift over a long day.

Starts on its own

Runs quietly at login, in the system tray or menu bar. Nothing to remember to open.

Pick what plays

Stream the whole computer, or just the apps you choose (Windows). You can also play a file or a device plugged into your PC.

A dashboard that shows what's happening

See which speakers are playing, your sync delay and a live level meter, with a sleep timer and one volume slider for everything.

Private

Runs entirely on your machine. No account, no cloud, no telemetry, and on Windows it never uses your microphone.

Set up in three steps

  1. Install. Run the Windows installer or open the Mac app. It sets up the free virtual audio driver it needs.
  2. Pick your speakers. The dashboard opens in your browser. Turn on the Sonos speakers you want.
  3. Play something and press Auto. PC2Sonos measures the delay, and your PC speakers and Sonos play together from then on.

Common questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There's no paid version, trial or license key. If it's useful to you, there's an optional pay-what-you-want donation.

Does it need the Sonos app or a Sonos account?

No. PC2Sonos talks to your speakers directly on your home network.

Can I just use Sonos as my computer speaker, without my PC speakers?

Yes. Leave the PC speaker volume at zero, or unplug them, and only Sonos plays.

Which Sonos speakers work?

Any Sonos speaker on your network. Turn on as many as you like and each gets its own volume.
