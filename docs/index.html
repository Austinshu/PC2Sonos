Set up

Three steps. Then forget it.

  1. 01

    Install

    Run the Windows installer or open the Mac app. It sets up the free virtual audio driver it needs.

  2. 02

    Pick your speakers

    The dashboard opens in your browser. Turn on the Sonos speakers you want.

  3. 03

    Play something, press Auto

    PC2Sonos measures the delay, and your PC speakers and Sonos play together from then on.

Sonos always plays a second or two behind your computer. Play both and you hear everything twice. PC2Sonos holds your desk speakers back by exactly that much, so they land together.

Why your PC speakers and Sonos echo

Mess it up.
Then fix it.

That delay is built into Sonos so a group of speakers stays in step, and no app can remove it. So PC2Sonos delays your computer instead, like the lip-sync setting on a TV. Press Auto once in the app and it measures the gap for you.

Echo gap0milliseconds

Flip the switch off to hear what your speakers sound like without PC2Sonos, then back on to fix it. Real Sonos lag is 1 to 2 seconds. The demo beat uses a shorter gap so you can hear the doubling.

What it does

Everything you'd want
from a desk rig.

Discovery

Every Sonos, found

Finds all your speakers automatically, even on a separate network or VLAN. Turn on the ones you want, each with its own volume.

Sync

No echo, ever

Your PC speakers are delayed to match Sonos, and it resyncs in the background so it doesn't drift over a long day.

Autostart

Starts on its own

Runs quietly at login, in the system tray or menu bar. Nothing to remember to open.

Sources

Pick what plays

Stream the whole computer, or just the apps you choose (Windows). You can also play a file or a device plugged into your PC.

Dashboard

See it all live

Which speakers are playing, your sync delay and a live level meter, with a sleep timer and one volume slider for everything.

Volume + EQ

Crank the bass

Each Sonos and each PC speaker gets its own volume slider, even headsets Windows can't turn down. Add bass, mid and treble EQ or a boost for quiet desk speakers.

Privacy

Yours alone

Runs entirely on your machine. No account, no cloud, no telemetry, and on Windows it never uses your microphone.

Common questions

Ask away.

More questions and answers →

Is it really free?

Yes. There's no paid version, trial or license key. If it's useful to you, there's an optional pay-what-you-want donation.

Does it need the Sonos app or a Sonos account?

No. PC2Sonos talks to your speakers directly on your home network.

Can I just use Sonos as my computer speaker, without my PC speakers?

Yes. Leave the PC speaker volume at zero, or unplug them, and only Sonos plays.

Which Sonos speakers work?

Any Sonos speaker on your network. Turn on as many as you like and each gets its own volume.
